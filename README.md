# Khata MiTtha Homepage Refresh

Create a premium, modern, highly responsive homepage for fresh juice brand “Khata MiTtha.” No reference poster attachment is available in this request, so derive the identity from the brief: vibrant orange, warm cream, dark brown/black, natural green; warm sunlight, fresh fruits, realistic premium juice bottles, natural textures; elegant, youthful, trustworthy Indian brand. Do not make it feel like a generic template.

Homepage first only, polished and complete. Include: clean nav with crafted text/logo treatment; editorial hero with a large premium realistic juice bottle and fresh fruit composition; headline exactly “Freshly Squeezed. Freshly Alive.”; concise brand description; highly prominent “Order Now” and “Explore Juices” CTAs. Add featured juice cards for Orange, Mosambi, Mango, Pineapple, and Mixed Fruit; Why Khata MiTtha section highlighting 100% Natural, No Sugar Added, Cold-Pressed Daily, Fresh Ingredients; a visual juice-making/freshness process; brand story; testimonials; strong final “Grab Yours Today” CTA; and footer. Use warm cream background, orange primary accent, subtle green support, rounded cards, soft shadows, whitespace, quality imagery, nuanced hover/scroll animation, and exceptionally simple mobile layout suitable for QR-code visitors. Make CTAs and basic nav interactions work; establish structure that will later accommodate shop, product, cart/checkout, and about pages. Focus effort on visual quality of the homepage.
